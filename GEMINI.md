# XCut AI

The `xcutai` MCP server researches and writes social media content for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook and the Meta Ad Library.

- Use it for what is going viral in a niche, a creator's or competitor's best posts, a post's transcript and why it worked, hooks, captions, bios, titles, scripts, carousels, ad copy and content calendars.
- Hooks, playbooks and quick writing tools work without an account. Tools that use canvases, brand memory, imports or credits ask the user to sign in to XCut AI once, in the browser.
- With no canvas named, imports and writing go to the user's "From AI apps" canvas. Do not ask which canvas.
- Do not use it for general web or news search. It never publishes to social media.
